About me

A teacher's eye for the why behind the numbers.

I'm Kishan, a Mathematics and I.T. teacher moving into data analytics. I teach 11th and 12th grade science, commerce and I.T. students at H.S International High School in Mumbai, and I've spent three years turning difficult ideas into explanations that land.

That is the same job an analyst does. Find the signal in the noise, strip away what doesn't matter, and hand someone a clear answer they can act on. I now do it with Power BI, SQL, Excel and Python instead of a whiteboard.
